# Information Architecture

## Primary navigation

  - Dashboard

  - Patients

  - Schedule

  - Follow-up

  - Payments

  - Reports

  - Administration

Clinical work is entered through patient context rather than a global “Clinical” module.

## Patient workspace

  - Overview

  - Appointments

  - Clinical chart

  - Periodontal chart

  - Treatment plans

  - Journeys and lab work

  - Documents

  - Ledger

  - Communications

  - Audit history, permission-controlled

## Dashboard by role

### Reception

  - Today’s arrivals

  - Unconfirmed appointments

  - Overdue contact tasks

  - Waitlist candidates

  - Lab-dependent appointments at risk

### Dentist

  - Today’s patients

  - Unsigned encounters

  - Active journeys requiring clinical decision

  - Treatment ready to schedule

  - Clinical follow-ups due

### Assistant

  - Preparation tasks

  - Lab orders due

  - Missing documents

  - Assigned journey tasks

### Office manager

  - Schedule utilization

  - Overdue follow-up

  - Day-end status

  - Patient balances

  - Security and operational exceptions

## Context preservation

  - Patient header remains stable across patient sections.

  - Switching modules does not discard drafts.

  - Search is globally available.

  - Short tasks open in side panels; complex work uses dedicated pages.

  - Breadcrumbs describe functional context, not implementation modules.

## Follow-up Center interaction

Users can filter by date, category, journey type, assignee, provider, priority, and state. Selecting a row opens a context panel that supports the next action, patient contact, task completion, or appointment creation without losing the queue.
